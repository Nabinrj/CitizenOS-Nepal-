# CitizenOS Nepal

CitizenOS Nepal is a proposed AI-powered Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) platform for Nepal that provides citizens with one secure digital gateway to government and authorized services.

The platform is designed around four core layers:

1. **Digital Identity** — trusted citizen authentication and verification.
2. **Consent & Data Exchange** — privacy-preserving interoperability between government agencies and authorized institutions.
3. **Service & Payment Orchestration** — unified workflows for public services, renewals, fees, insurance checks, and payments.
4. **Citizen Applications & AI** — digital documents, notifications, benefit discovery, job matching, and an AI Citizen Assistant.

## Core Principles

- One trusted identity, not one giant database.
- Government agencies remain authoritative owners of their records.
- Consent-based and purpose-limited data sharing.
- Secure digital credentials and auditability by design.
- Human oversight for high-impact government decisions.
- Mobile-first, accessible, and resilient for Nepal's connectivity realities.
- Build as national digital public infrastructure, not just another government portal.

## Initial MVP Scope

The reference implementation will prove the architecture through a focused set of realistic flows:

- Verified digital document vault
- Driving licence and vehicle renewal
- Government payment workflow
- Insurance verification
- Education credential verification
- Government benefit discovery
- AI Citizen Assistant

## Project Positioning

This repository is a **reference architecture and working prototype** for how a citizen-centered digital public infrastructure platform could be designed for Nepal. It does not claim to be an official government system or to replace existing government authorities.
